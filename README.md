# Legal IPTV and Content Licensing

IPTV stands for **Internet Protocol Television**.

It is a technology for delivering television and video over internet networks.

The technology itself is used by many legitimate television and streaming platforms.

However, the legal status of content depends on whether the service has appropriate rights to distribute it in a particular region.

Before purchasing an independent IPTV subscription, consider checking:

- Provider identity
- Terms of service
- Content licensing information
- Privacy policy
- Refund terms
- Payment security
- Regional restrictions
- Local regulations

A free trial demonstrates how a service performs technically. It does **not** by itself demonstrate that the service has distribution rights for every channel in its lineup.
